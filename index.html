Research vision · Human–computer interaction

Beyond Design Outcomes

Interactive Tools for Design Reasoning

My research investigates how interactive tools can support design reasoning as problems and solutions co-evolve.

I focus on CAD and digital fabrication, where design decisions shape geometry, materials, fabrication processes, and physical use. Through comparison, reflection, and collaboration, I develop tools that help designers explore alternatives, recognize emerging considerations, and continue developing unresolved decisions.

Research question · How can interactive design tools support designers' reasoning as problems and solutions co-evolve?

I organize this research around two dimensions: what designers reason about across the Problem and Solution Spaces, and who participates in developing a design decision, from individuals to groups and broader communities.

Published ACM CHI · 2026

CAMeleon: Comparing Fabrication Workflows in CAD to Support Design Reasoning

Shuo Feng, Xuening Wang, Yifan (Lavenda) Shan, Krista U Singh, Bo Liu, Amritansh Kwatra, Ritik Batra, Tobias M Weinberg, Thijs Roumen

Learners often begin with a familiar fabrication workflow and may lack the knowledge to judge alternatives. Early modeling choices can make it difficult to change direction as their design develops. CAMeleon lets learners compare workflow outcomes for the same model, supporting exploration of trade-offs and the development of informed design judgments.

aCADemy: Guided Reflection on Fabrication-centric CAD Decisions Through AI

aCADemy is an AI-based reflective interface that works alongside CAD software. It presents contextual questions about fabrication considerations during modeling, helping designers reflect on decisions while retaining control over how their designs develop.

FuzzyCAD: Supporting Collaborative Design Reasoning through Deferred Decisions in CAD

FuzzyCAD lets collaborators express unresolved design decisions directly on CAD geometry, contribute constraints and identify competing requirements. The project examines how collaborators with different expertise can progressively develop these decisions across asynchronous handoffs.

Applied research Siegel PiTech Fellowship · 2026

Making Assistive Technology Search Actionable through a Multi-Source Retrieval Tool for TOM

People seeking assistive technology often describe everyday challenges without knowing the technical names of relevant solutions. Developed with TOM Global, this tool connects conversational intake to searches across assistive technology catalogs, open-source designs, and commercial products. It helps staff assess whether a result can be used as-is, adapted, or turned into a new maker challenge when existing solutions do not fit.

Published ACM SCF · 2025

Y-AR: A Mixed Reality CAD Tool for 3D Wire Bending

Shuo Feng, Bo Liu, Yifan (Lavenda) Shan, Roy Zunder, Wei-Che Lin, Tri Dinh, Harald Haraldsson, Ofer Berman, Thijs Roumen

Y-AR is a mixed-reality CAD tool for creating three-dimensional wire structures that connect to objects in the physical environment. Its interface incorporates springs as design primitives, both to apply forces that hold objects and to accommodate dimensional inaccuracies from mid-air modeling and measurement.

Published ACM SCF · 2025

OriStitch: A Machine Embroidery Workflow to Turn Existing Fabrics into Self-Folding 3D Textiles

Zekun Chang, Yixuan Gao, Yuta Noma, Shuo Feng, Xinyi Yang, Kazuhiro Shinoda, Tung D Ta, Koji Yatani, Tomoyuki Yokota, Takao Someya, Tomohiro Tachi, Yoshihiro Kawahara, Koya Narumi, Francois Guimbretiere, Thijs Roumen

OriStitch transforms existing flat fabrics into self-folding three-dimensional structures through computational embroidery. Functional threads are stitched in specific patterns and then activated with heat to achieve a target form. The workflow works with a range of materials, including leather, woven fabric, and denim.

Published International Journal of Human–Computer Studies · 2024

Design with Myself: A Brain–Computer Interface Design Tool That Predicts Live Emotion to Enhance Metacognitive Monitoring of Designers

Qi Yang, Shuo Feng, Tianlin Zhao, Saleh Kalantari

Multi-Self is a BCI–VR design tool that provides real-time visual feedback about designers' emotional responses to support metacognitive monitoring. A pilot study with 24 participants reported increased design exploration and self-awareness, while responses to the accuracy of the emotional feedback were mixed.

Published ACADIA · 2023

Emotive Room: A BCI Interactive Architectural Interface

Shuo Feng, Saleh Kalantari, Jenny Sabin

Emotive Room explores how an interactive architectural environment can respond to people's emotional states through real-time brain–computer interaction. By changing spatial color and form in response to stress-related feedback, the project investigates responsive environments that may support calmness and well-being.
