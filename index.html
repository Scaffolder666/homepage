About me

I am a Ph.D. student in Information Science at Cornell Tech, advised by Prof. Thijs Roumen. I study how interactive design tools support people's reasoning while they design and fabricate physical objects.

My research explores CAD, digital fabrication, human–AI interaction, and interdisciplinary collaboration. I build systems that help people understand design decisions, express uncertainty, compare alternatives, and reflect on the consequences of their choices.

Before my Ph.D., I studied Matter Design Computation at Cornell University and architecture at SCI-Arc. My work connects human-computer interaction, computational design, and fabrication.
