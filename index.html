01 / About

Connecting design thinking and computational tools.

I study how interactive systems can make design decisions easier to understand, explore, and communicate. My work brings together human-computer interaction, computer-aided design, and physical fabrication.

Before my PhD, I studied Matter Design Computation at Cornell University and architecture at SCI-Arc. This interdisciplinary background informs how I build tools for designers and makers.
