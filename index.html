Human–computer interaction · Cornell Tech

Design tools for reasoning, making, and collaboration.

I'm a Ph.D. student in Information Science at Cornell Tech, advised by Prof. Thijs Roumen. I research interactive design tools that help people reason about decisions while designing and fabricating physical objects.

My work spans CAD, digital fabrication, human–AI interaction, and interdisciplinary collaboration. I build tools for exploring alternatives, expressing uncertainty, and reflecting on design consequences.

Previously, I studied Matter Design Computation at Cornell University and architecture at SCI-Arc.
