About Me

I design interactive tools that support design reasoning and help people make informed decisions when creating physical objects.

I am a PhD student in Information Science at Cornell Tech, advised by Prof. Thijs Roumen. My research focuses on human–computer interaction, computer-aided design (CAD), and digital fabrication. I study how design tools can help people articulate uncertainty, explore alternatives, and reflect on the decisions behind their models.

My background spans architecture and computational design. Before starting my PhD, I earned an M.S. in Matter Design Computation at Cornell University and an M.Arch. from SCI-Arc.

Research interests: HCI · design reasoning · creativity support tools · human–AI interaction · digital fabrication.

Ongoing Research

aCADemy · Reflection during CAD modeling
