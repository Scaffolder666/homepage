A Exam · Storyboard · Working page

Present the agenda without changing its logic

这页以当前 Research Agenda 为骨架。Thijs Roumen 的 Portable Laser Cutting talk 只作为 presentation rhetoric 的参考，用来学习什么时候先给 concrete example、什么时候 zoom out、什么时候命名概念、什么时候 checkpoint。它不决定我的 research-program structure。

Current agenda spineDesign understanding develops together with possible solutions → designers make decisions while both are still developing → CAD mediates many of these decisions → supporting reasoning requires knowing both what the designer is reasoning about and what form of reasoning can help → their intersection defines a Design Reasoning Space → current projects investigate selected intersections.

01 · What is and is not borrowed

Thijs provides the delivery pattern, not the research structure

Borrow

Concrete before abstract. Let the audience see a phenomenon before naming it. Move broad → narrow → concrete. Make every slide create the need for the next. Revisit the central question after a major section. Put technical depth after the motivation is already clear. Close by returning to the opening question or artifact.

Do not borrow

Sequential project dependency. Portable Laser Cutting can be narrated as one engineering problem exposing the next. aCADemy, CAMeleon, and FuzzyCAD are not a chain in which the previous project fails and causes the next. They are parallel investigations of different regions in a larger research space.

02 · Agenda logic → presentation move

The conceptual progression stays the same

这里每一步都来自现有 agenda。右栏只处理“怎么讲”，不改变左栏的理论关系。

01Broad

Design understanding develops together with possible solutions

Agenda claim

Design problems cannot be fully specified before designing begins. Goals, requirements, constraints, and possible solutions develop together.

Presentation move

Show one evolving design first.不要先念 wicked problems。用一个设计例子展示：最初目标很简单，随着 modeling / feedback / constraints 进入，designer 对“这个设计应该是什么”的理解发生变化。然后再给一句总结。
02Narrow

Designers therefore make decisions while understanding is still developing

Agenda claim

Design reasoning does not finish before modeling starts. It continues alongside the developing design.

Presentation move

Turn the evolving example into decisions.把刚才的 design evolution 标成 3–4 个 decision moments。让 committee 看到“design evolves”不是抽象理论，而是 decision 不断被形成、修改、重开。
03Setting

CAD mediates many of these decisions

Agenda claim

CAD gives designers representations through which they specify, inspect, and modify a design. This makes CAD an important site for supporting design reasoning.

Presentation move

Now enter CAD.先承认 CAD 已经非常擅长 precise geometry。这样后面的 critique 不是“CAD 不好”，而是“它很好地表示 geometry，但 reasoning support 还有空间”。
04Blocker 1

A precise CAD model does not necessarily reveal what the designer is reasoning about

Agenda claim

The same geometric representation can respond to different design concerns. A dimension or placement may reflect a requirement, criterion, constraint, or possible solution.

Presentation move

Use the same geometry, change the meaning.一张 dimension / placement 图不动，依次出现四种可能的 design concern。Audience 会直接理解为什么 geometry alone 不够。
05Question 1

What are designers reasoning about?

First dimension

Goals / Requirements · Criteria · Constraints · Possible Solutions.

Presentation move

Name the first dimension only after Blocker 1 is visible.这时候 classification 是解决刚才那个 ambiguity 的工具，不是开场要 audience 记的一套 taxonomy。
06Blocker 2

Knowing the concern is still not enough

Agenda claim

The same concern can be developed through different forms of reasoning. A constraint can trigger reflection, knowledge retrieval, analogy, comparison, consequence reasoning, or prototyping.

Presentation move

Hold the concern constant, vary the reasoning.选一个 constraint，不换 design concern，只展示六种可能的 reasoning action。这样第二个维度和第一个维度真正独立。
07Question 2

What forms of reasoning help designers?

Second dimension

Reflection · Knowledge Retrieval · Analogical Reasoning · Comparison · Consequence Reasoning · Prototyping.

Presentation move

Name the second dimension after the audience sees why one axis is insufficient.此时才有“×”的必要性。
08Synthesis

The Design Reasoning Space

Agenda structure

Crossing the two dimensions defines families of research questions about how interactive CAD tools can support a particular form of reasoning around a particular design concern.

Presentation move

Reveal the matrix progressively.先显示 rows，再显示 columns，最后交叉成 4 × 6。不要一上来甩完整表格。
09Current work

Three projects investigate three intersections

Current probes

aCADemy = Criteria × Reflection. CAMeleon = Possible Solutions × Comparison. FuzzyCAD = Possible Solutions × Knowledge Retrieval.

Presentation move

Projects appear after the space exists.三个项目是 parallel probes。每个 chapter 都从一个 cell 的 research question 开始，再讲 system、study、evidence、takeaway，然后回到 matrix 标记这个 cell。
10Future

The remaining space generates future research

Agenda generativity

The empty regions identify questions beyond current projects. A second future direction concerns how reasoning moves across concerns, representations, people, and time.

Presentation move

Do not read empty cells one by one.选 2–3 个有 dissertation potential 的 regions 或 transitions。结尾回到最开始那个 evolving design，说明 CAD 未来可以支持的不只是 geometry，还包括 design development 本身。

05 · Project chapter rhythm

三个项目平行，不串成 “A 不够 → B → C”

aCADemy · Criteria × Reflection

cell question → concrete criterion problem → reflective interaction → study → evidence → what this cell teaches us → return to matrix.

CAMeleon · Possible Solutions × Comparison

cell question → alternative realization problem → comparison interaction → study → evidence → what this cell teaches us → return to matrix.

FuzzyCAD · Possible Solutions × Knowledge Retrieval

cell question → unresolved solution depends on distributed expertise → handoff interaction → study → evidence → what this cell teaches us → return to matrix.

06 · What still needs work

接下来重点磨这些，不急着增加更多 slides

Opening artifact

最好选一个能同时支持“understanding develops”“CAD mediates decisions”“same geometry different concern”的例子。crawling trainer 很可能最合适。

Definition of informed

需要保持 agenda 的含义，但在 talk 中压成一句 committee 可以一直记住的 working definition。

Dimension 1 boundary

Goals / Requirements 和 Criteria / Constraints 的边界还要准备 Q&A backup，不一定在主 deck 展开。

Future story

不要把 future 变成空格清单。更有力的是选若干 high-value intersections，再补一个跨 cell 的问题：reasoning 如何随 design development 移动。
