A Exam · Storyboard · Working page

How to tell the A Exam story

这页不是 research-agenda 正文，也不是最终 slides。它只处理一件事：committee 为什么会愿意跟着下一页走。左边按时间顺序拆解 Thijs Roumen 在 Portable Laser Cutting seminar 里的实际讲法，右边给出我在 A Exam 中承担同一种 rhetorical job 的页面。

核心原则是保留他的叙事机制，不复制他的技术依赖关系。Portable Laser Cutting 可以天然写成 2D → 3D → legacy reconstruction → automation。我的三个 projects 不是前一个失败后才产生下一个，所以它们要由一个更大的 research promise 串起来。

Current working promiseCAD makes geometric decisions explicit. My research asks how CAD can support the reasoning designers need while those decisions are still developing.

01 · What to learn from Thijs

不是模仿句子，是模仿 audience 的认知路径

Concrete object first先让 audience 看见一个 artifact 和具体困难，再把问题命名。
Create a promise整个 talk 有一个 audience 可以不断拿回来检查的目标。
Define the central idea earlyThijs 很早定义 portability。我的 talk 也要很早说明什么叫 informed design reasoning。
Plant open loops在讲 project 之前先告诉 audience 还有哪些问题没有解决，让后面的 project 有必然性。
Checkpoint after each project先承认这一部分解决了，再回到更大的 promise，看还缺什么。
Let the audience experience a failure能用一个具体 task 让 committee 自己发现困难，就不要先给抽象结论。
Reality breaks the clean solution下一步不是因为上一项研究“失败”，而是现实条件暴露出新的 reasoning demand。
Return to the opening结尾重新出现最开始的 artifact / promise，让整个 talk 闭环。
Agenda must generate next questionsQ&A 里最强的回答不是列 future work，而是用顶层 aspiration 推出下一层 abstraction。

Act I · 先让 audience 看见问题

Thijs 1:50–9:20 · Shuo Slides 1–6
011:50–2:25

从一个看起来已经很好的 artifact 开始

Thijs

“Laser-cut files look like this… in many ways, this is a great format. But my talk today is about why this will have to die.”

Rhetorical job 先建立 familiar object，再制造 contradiction。Audience 会自然想知道“它明明不错，为什么要死？”
→

A Exam · Slide 1

A precise CAD model can still hide an unfinished reasoning process

“This model tells us exactly what geometry was chosen.”

“But from the geometry alone, we cannot tell whether the designer considered the relevant criteria, compared plausible alternatives, or had the knowledge needed to commit.”

Visual 一张你最干净的 CAD final model。只标 2–3 个 consequential decisions，不放 framework。
022:25–4:12

从具体 artifact 暂时 zoom out 到一个更远的未来

Thijs

他转向 computing history，先讲 millions → billions users 对应 mobility，再用 mobile fabrication / broken bike lamp 展示一个 2–3 decades away 的 vision。

Rhetorical job 先给 audience 一个值得努力的 future，再承认它还太远。
→

A Exam · Slide 2

CAD should support more than the specification of geometry

“CAD has become extremely good at helping designers specify, inspect, and modify geometry.”

“My longer-term goal is for the CAD environment to also support the reasoning through which designers decide how that geometry should develop.”

Visual 左边 current CAD operations，右边 developing reasoning。不要上 taxonomy。
034:12–5:10

把远未来收回来：我们现在到底处在哪一步？

Thijs

他说 fabrication 还没有到 billions。当前更像从 thousands → millions，于是追问 computing 当年这一跳靠什么完成。

Rhetorical job 把 aspiration 缩成一个当前可研究的 transition。
→

A Exam · Slide 3

The immediate problem is not whether CAD can represent a decision

“The geometry is already representable.”

“The immediate gap is whether the considerations needed to form that decision can enter the design process while the decision is still developing.”

Visual 同一 CAD decision 左边是 explicit geometry，右边是 criteria / alternatives / knowledge 尚未进入 reasoning。
045:10–6:39

很早定义整场 talk 的核心概念

Thijs

compiler → hardware abstraction → standards → portability。他给出一句可重复使用的定义：portability 是从一个 machine/system 转到另一个。

Rhetorical job 给 audience 一把尺子，之后每个 project 都可以问：这有没有推进 portability？
→

A Exam · Slide 4

What do I mean by informed design reasoning?

“I do not use informed to mean that a designer has complete information or reaches an objectively correct outcome.”

“I mean that relevant considerations can enter the reasoning while a decision is being formed.”

“Those considerations may include criteria, alternatives, consequences, evidence, uncertainty, or expertise.”

Need to refine 这页要继续磨成你自己的 portability-definition moment。它必须短，且后面每个 project 都能回来检查。
056:44–8:53

回到 artifact，把大问题拆成 audience 看得见的小问题

Thijs

回到 microscope。他依次指出 joint fit、moving mechanism、以及更大的 parametric modification 三类问题。前两类对应“PDF-like portability”，第三类对应 underlying editable representation。

Rhetorical job 在任何 project 出现前，先把 larger promise 分解成若干 concrete blockers。
→

A Exam · Slide 5

Three ways a CAD decision can still lack what it needs

“A decision can be geometrically explicit while a relevant criterion has not entered attention.”

“Several possible solutions can remain plausible without a useful basis for comparison.”

“A solution can remain unresolved because the relevant knowledge or expertise is elsewhere.”

Visual 三个具体 CAD moments，分别对应 aCADemy、CAMeleon、FuzzyCAD。此时只讲现象，不讲项目名。
068:53–9:20

Plant an open loop：先告诉 audience 我现在只解决其中一部分

Thijs

“Let's look at these first two issues first.” 更大的 modification problem 被明确留到后面。

Rhetorical job Audience 已经知道后面还有一笔账没结，所以 project 之间不会像论文列表。
→

A Exam · Slide 6

I started with the first failure: a relevant criterion may never enter attention

“I first looked at what happens when the issue is not yet part of the designer's active reasoning.”

“This became the starting point for aCADemy.”

Then insert aCADemy system → study → evidence，约 3–4 slides。

Act II · Project 1 之后一定 checkpoint

Thijs 9:20–16:52 · Shuo Slides 7–9 + evidence
079:20–15:28

第一个 technical block 只解决它承诺的局部问题

Thijs

SpringFit / kerf-canceling mechanisms 把 machine/material variation 吸收进 cutting plan。讲完后展示多个 model 在不同材料中仍能工作。

Rhetorical job 用 system + mechanism + examples 让 audience 真正相信这一小块已经解决。
→

A Exam · Slides 7–8

aCADemy · Criteria × Reflection

“aCADemy introduces situated reflective questions during modeling.”

“The questions do not prescribe a solution. They give relevant fabrication criteria a way to enter the designer's reasoning.”

Evidence slides interaction → N=12 study → 211 question appearances / Not Now / later enacted / ownership & reasoning support。
0815:28–16:52

先承认成功，再回到最初 promise 看还欠什么

Thijs

“I promised you portability… we sort of achieved that. So great. Right. But… the bigger thing here is I sort of promised you something more.”

Rhetorical job Project 1 没有失败。它完成了自己的任务，但 overarching promise 还没完成。
→

A Exam · Slide 9

Reflection can surface criteria. The broader reasoning problem remains.

“This gives us one answer: CAD can prompt reflection when relevant criteria may be overlooked.”

“But noticing what matters does not tell a designer how different possible solutions compare against those considerations.”

Transition 这不是说 aCADemy 有 limitation，而是把 audience 带回 Slide 5 里尚未解决的第二类 problem。

Act III · 让 audience 自己经历第二个问题

Thijs 16:52–22:13 · Shuo Slides 10–12 + evidence
0916:52–20:17

不要先解释 difficulty，让 audience 亲自做一次

Thijs

他给 VR headset modification task，让 audience 判断哪些 2D plates 要改。最后指出大家实际上先 mentally reconstruct 3D，再 imagine modification，再 project back to 2D。

Rhetorical job Problem is demonstrated before it is named.
→

A Exam · Slide 10

Which of these fabrication directions should we develop?

“Suppose the same model can be fabricated through several plausible workflows.”

“Which direction should we develop, and what would you need to know before choosing?”

Audience task 给 committee 一个具体 model + 2–3 fabrication approaches。不要一上来给 comparison theory。
1020:17–22:13

系统在 audience 已经理解需求之后才出现

Thijs

“I can't help but wonder what it would be like if you had that model in 3D in the first place.”

然后才展示 Cube 3D editor、parametric modification 和 machine-specific export。

Rhetorical job Solution feels inevitable because audience just experienced the representational mismatch.
→

A Exam · Slides 11–12

CAMeleon · Possible Solutions × Comparison

“CAMeleon keeps alternative design-to-fabrication approaches available in one model context.”

“The interaction makes their fabrication implications and trade-offs available for comparison before a direction is selected.”

Evidence slides interaction → educator interviews → learner evaluation → 2–3 concrete comparison trajectories。

Act IV · Clean solution meets the real world

Thijs 22:13–32:42 · Shuo Slides 13–15 + evidence
1122:13–22:31

第二次 checkpoint：故意让 audience 觉得“这下总该结束了吧”

Thijs

“All these models are portable, they're parametric and everything is shared. So in many ways, we're done here, right?”

Rhetorical job 先建立一个 clean solution，后面的 real-world constraint 才有冲击力。
→

A Exam · Slide 13

Reflection and comparison still assume the needed knowledge is available here

“At this point, we can surface criteria and compare possible solutions.”

“But both interactions assume that the designer has access to the knowledge needed to continue the decision.”

1222:31–24:19

Reality invalidates the clean solution

Thijs

大量 valuable models 仍是 legacy 2D cutting plans。Researcher 可以说“大家都改用 3D”，但 standards stick。他用 guitar tabs 说明 legacy format 为什么不会自己消失，再用 study 数据说明 modification 的实际成本。

Rhetorical job 下一项研究不是补前一项技术漏洞，而是现实条件提出了新的 requirement。
→

A Exam · Slide 14

In collaboration, the knowledge needed to resolve a solution may belong to someone else

“A mechanical designer can recognize that a support dimension is unresolved without having the clinical knowledge needed to settle it.”

“A therapist can know what movement must remain possible without being able to validate structural stability.”

“The question becomes how that unresolved decision survives until the relevant expertise can enter.”

Visual 直接用 crawling-trainer chain。Designer / mechanical / PT，每个人各有 partial knowledge。
1324:19–30:30

第三个系统解决“如何把现有 representation 带到可继续工作的状态”

Thijs

Assembler 把 2D cutting plans reconstruct 回 3D。先 interactive assembly，再进入可 parametric modification 的环境。

Rhetorical job System 不是孤立 feature，而是维护 larger promise 与现实存量之间的 continuity。
→

A Exam · Slide 15 + evidence

FuzzyCAD · Possible Solutions × Knowledge Retrieval

“FuzzyCAD allows parts of a developing solution to remain explicitly unresolved in the CAD model.”

“The unresolved decision carries its constraints, alternatives, and needed input into later handoffs, where another collaborator can contribute relevant expertise.”

Evidence slides N=9 / three chains → walkthrough → decision trajectories → unresolved end states。
1430:42–32:01

展示 breadth，让 audience 相信 solution 不是只适用于一个 demo

Thijs

快速展示 100 个 online models 被 reconstruct，强调 variation 和 parametric reuse。

Rhetorical job 从 mechanism proof 转到 research claim 的 breadth。
→

A Exam · Evidence slide

Across chains, unresolved decisions were refined by different forms of expertise

“The important result is not that every decision became resolved.”

“Across handoffs, collaborators could contribute what they knew, preserve what remained uncertain, and leave a more specific problem for the next person.”

04 · Open story decisions

接下来真正需要一起磨的不是更多 slides，而是这几句话

1 · The promise

“CAD makes geometric decisions explicit. My research asks how CAD can support the reasoning designers need while those decisions are still developing.” 这句已经能工作，但还要继续看是不是你的 “portability”。

2 · Definition of informed

要做到像 portability 一样短、稳定、后面每个 project 都能回来检查。现在的 working definition 是 relevant considerations enter reasoning while the decision is being formed。

3 · The three concrete failures

Criteria overlooked / alternatives difficult to compare / relevant knowledge elsewhere。要确保它们不是为了装三个 projects 现造的，而是真正从 larger agenda 推出来。

4 · When the matrix appears

当前建议是三个 projects 之后才出现。它承担 synthesis 和 generativity，不承担 opening motivation。

5 · The closing future

最好只选两类：open cells + movement across the space。不要把 future work 讲成 21 个 empty cells。

6 · Audience participation

需要找一个像 Thijs VR-headset task 一样，30–60 秒内就能让 committee 亲自感受到 reasoning gap 的 CAD example。
