How might we support design reasoning in CAD?

这一页专门用来规划 A Exam 的 presentation。初版按 42 分钟、30 张主 slides 编排。每一页都要承担一个具体任务：给一个例子、解释一个 interaction、报告一组 evidence、或者把三个 projects 接回同一条 research agenda。

Talk spine

Design decisions enter CAD with different epistemic conditions. I study how CAD interactions can surface overlooked decisions, develop grounds for unresolved decisions, preserve decisions that need more input, and bring relevant knowledge into the model.

06

1.1 · What is holding back informed design reasoning?

Source section

holding-back

Exact agenda paragraph

However, a precise CAD model does not necessarily reveal what the designer is reasoning about. CAD tools can require designers to specify geometry precisely even while their intentions remain ambiguous or exploratory (Gross & Do, 1996). The model records the resulting geometry, but the same geometric representation can reflect different design concerns. A specific dimension or spatial placement may respond to a functional requirement that the design must accomplish, an evaluation criterion used to judge a design choice, a physical constraint that bounds possible solutions, or a possible solution being explored. These concerns require different information to be developed before a designer can make an informed decision. Yet this distinction is not necessarily visible in the geometry itself. Consequently, design intent and the reasoning behind prior modeling actions can be difficult to interpret from the resulting CAD artifact or workflow alone (Cheng et al., 2026).

1.1 · What is holding back informed design reasoning?

Source section

holding-back

Exact agenda paragraph

Knowing what a decision concerns is also not sufficient to support it. Designers can address the same design concern through different forms of reasoning. To address a constraint, a designer might reflect on the current design, retrieve relevant domain knowledge, draw from an analogous case, compare alternative directions, anticipate consequences, or generate new evidence through prototyping (Schön, 1983; MacLean et al., 1991; Ball & Christensen, 2019). These different forms of reasoning can call for different forms of tool support. Current CAD tools provide extensive support for specifying and modifying geometry, while these forms of reasoning receive less direct support.

从 Thijs 的 dissertation 学 deck 怎么立起来

Thijs Roumen 的 dissertation Portable models for laser cutting ↗ 很适合作为结构参考。他先提出一个 field-level goal，再指出一个具体 blocker，随后给出 central thesis，并用三个技术方向推进同一个目标：让 2D cutting plans 对 machine variation 更稳健、用 3D representation 支持修改与复用、把 legacy 2D plans 转回 3D。最后一章重新回到总目标并展开 future outlook。

我可以直接借用的结构习惯

  1. 先讲一个具体问题。 听众先看到一个 model 里的几个 consequential decisions，理解为什么 CAD 里会出现 reasoning gap。
  2. 很早给出 central question。 How might we support design reasoning in CAD?
  3. projects 作为同一问题的三次推进。 aCADemy 研究 overlooked decisions；CAMeleon 研究已识别但缺少比较 grounds 的 decisions；FuzzyCAD 研究需要更多 expertise 的 unresolved decisions。
  4. 每个 project 都用同一套节奏。 concrete decision → interaction → study → evidence → 对 agenda 增加了什么。
  5. 结尾回到 program。 三个 projects 合起来说明 CAD 可以承载 reasoning；future work 继续研究 distributed knowledge 和 mechanism timing。

04 · Writing and delivery rules

每页怎么写

一页一个 claim标题直接写这一页要成立的句子。
先放 object，再放 concept先让 committee 看到 model、decision、interaction、study evidence，再说 epistemic state 或 mechanism。
沿用 paper wordingdesign decision、unresolved decision、defer commitment、comparison、reflection、geometry、collaborator、relevant expertise / evidence。
每个 project 用同一节奏decision → interaction → study → evidence → agenda takeaway。
数字直接放 slideN、chains、question counts、study conditions、tasks 都写在画面里，口头讲解释。
transition 说清楚下一页为什么出现每章结尾用一句话把 project 的结果接回 research question。
