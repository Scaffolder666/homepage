Research notes · Interaction precedents · 2020–2026

Interaction Precedents for Design Reasoning

A cross-domain reading collection of 26 interactive research systems from CHI, UIST, DIS, IUI, SCF, and TEI. Each paper is organized by the specific decision difficulty its interaction may help address. Open a paper to see what the user actually does and what could transfer to CAD. These are proposed analogies, not author-validated placements in this working framework.

Not recognized · Sufficient grounds · 6 papers

Discover a decision

Look for mechanisms that reveal a previously overlooked dimension or decision when relevant references, criteria, or knowledge are already accessible. Introducing a novel idea by itself does not establish decision awareness.

UIST 2021 · SymbolsSymbolFinder

Interaction: Start with an abstract design concept, traverse localized semantic associations, and inspect visual-symbol alternatives.

CAD transfer: Select a component and unfold relevant but overlooked functions or design considerations from a structured association network. A suggestion is an entry point, not proof of an actionable decision.

SymbolFinder: Brainstorming Diverse Symbols Using Local Semantic Networks ↗
CHI 2024 · Graphic designCreativeConnect

Interaction: Import reference images, inspect extracted concept words, explore related concepts, select combinations, and see recombined sketch ideas.

CAD transfer: Decompose a reference model into separately selectable concerns so unconsidered dimensions can surface before geometric refinement.

CreativeConnect: Supporting Reference Recombination for Graphic Design Ideation with Generative AI ↗
CHI 2025 · UI designMisty

Interaction: Bring a region of a reference interface into a draft, choose which attributes to blend (such as layout, color, or content), inspect a semantic difference, and toggle local edits.

CAD transfer: Make previously bundled design dimensions individually selectable, revealing a choice that the current model obscured.

Misty: UI Prototyping Through Interactive Conceptual Blending ↗
CHI 2024 · WritingImpressona

Interaction: Define an audience persona, select a passage, request feedback from that simulated perspective, then decide what to revise.

CAD transfer: Offer selectable assessment perspectives to expose a neglected design concern. An AI-generated persona is not an actual patient, manufacturer, or expert, and cannot replace their evidence.

Writer-Defined AI Personas for On-Demand Feedback Generation ↗
IUI 2023 · Co-drawingReframer

Interaction: Draw synchronously with an AI partner; compare interaction styles that emphasize emergent system behavior or direct user control, and revise the sketch in real time.

CAD transfer: Use an unexpected sketch development as a prompt to notice a new design consideration without assigning the model authority over the choice.

Drawing with Reframer: Emergence and Control in Co-Creative AI ↗
TEI 2023 · Tangible cardsTTP Toolkit

Interaction: Use a structured deck of ideation cards to generate tangible privacy-management concepts across different considerations.

CAD transfer: Present physical or digital perspective cards during modeling to make neglected requirements available for consideration. Placement here concerns issue discovery, not demonstrated sufficiency of design grounds.

A Card-based Ideation Toolkit to Generate Designs for Tangible Privacy Management Tools ↗

Mechanism to brainstorm: component selection → reveal a different criterion, reference relationship, or stakeholder perspective → user decides whether to record a new decision point.

Not recognized · Insufficient grounds · 8 papers

Surface a decision and missing grounds

Find situated critiques, disciplinary lenses, and scaffolds that reveal both an overlooked issue and what knowledge or evidence is needed next. The mapping is conditional: a tool belongs here only when the relevant issue was previously unrecognized.

UIST 2024 · Accessible web designDesignChecker

Interaction: Choose design guidelines or reference websites; receive findings attached to concrete page elements and inspect CSS-level suggestions.

CAD transfer: Attach a constraint violation and the relevant rule to geometry; separately flag unverified assumptions instead of silently applying a fix.

DesignChecker: Visual Design Support for Blind and Low Vision Web Developers ↗
UIST 2025 · DrawingArtKrit

Interaction: Work through composition, value, and color stages with reference-aligned feedback at each stage.

CAD transfer: Decompose an unfamiliar evaluation task into visible, testable dimensions and progressive critiques. The study concerns reference drawing; its feedback is not a universal creativity score.

Computational Scaffolding of Composition, Value, and Color for Disciplined Drawing ↗
CHI 2025 · Academic writingCorpusStudio

Interaction: Inspect structural patterns from an existing paper corpus; retrieve comparable sentences while writing, open source context, and save annotated examples.

CAD transfer: Show provenance-bearing precedents beside a localized design question, helping designers discover an unfamiliar requirement or missing evaluation convention.

CorpusStudio: Surfacing Emergent Patterns in a Corpus of Prior Work While Writing ↗
CHI 2024 · AR / spatial safetyRASSAR

Interaction: Scan a room with a mobile device; examine potential accessibility and safety findings anchored in AR; confirm or dismiss detections.

CAD transfer: Select a three-dimensional problem marker → inspect its applicable requirement and evidence gap → confirm, reject, or request review.

RASSAR: Room Accessibility and Safety Scanning in Augmented Reality ↗
CHI 2025 · Design reflectionFive Whys / Competitive Analysis whiteboard plugin

Interaction: Carry out staged why-questions and compare competing solutions within a shared design workflow, including reflective use of AI.

CAD transfer: When an assumption goes unnoticed, prompt for the underlying reason and what evidence would validate it before generating geometry.

Productive vs. Reflective: How Different Ways of Integrating AI into Design Workflows Affect Cognition and Motivation ↗
IUI 2024 · Accessible writingFigurA11y

Interaction: Generate an editable alt-text draft from figure and paper metadata, inspect contextual suggestions, and revise using accessibility guidance.

CAD transfer: Combine a concrete artifact, relevant standards, and contextual source material when surfacing an unfamiliar requirement. AI drafts are not validated ground truth.

FigurA11y: AI Assistance for Writing Scientific Alt Text ↗
IUI 2024 · IdeationJamplate

Interaction: Work in structured digital-whiteboard templates where individual contributions, LLM outputs, and in-situ reflection questions are organized together.

CAD transfer: Use geometry-linked reflection templates to expose an unrecognized assumption, elicit missing grounds, and retain both user and AI contributions for inspection.

Jamplate: Exploring LLM-Enhanced Templates for Idea Reflection ↗
TEI 2023 · Tangible ideationMix & Match Machine Learning

Interaction: Combine physical tokens representing data types and ML capabilities; use the associated web interface to explore capabilities and limitations without programming expertise.

CAD transfer: Externalize unfamiliar fabrication or disciplinary knowledge as manipulable capability/constraint objects. This quadrant applies when the designer initially has not even recognized that the issue matters.

Mix and Match Machine Learning: An Ideation Toolkit to Design Machine Learning-Enabled Solutions ↗

Mechanism to brainstorm: localized issue → explain the criterion and why it matters → expose unverified parameters, evidence, or expertise → user confirms or dismisses the issue.

Recognized · Insufficient grounds · 12 papers

Gather grounds or defer a decision

Look for interfaces that hold provisional constraints, make alternative consequences comparable, retrieve expertise and provenance, and allow a choice to stay open. Showing multiple candidates is not itself evidence that a decision can be made.

CHI 2020 · Layout explorationScout

Interaction: Define high-level interface layout constraints, generate multiple alternatives, inspect the resulting configurations, and iterate requirements.

CAD transfer: Let users articulate partial constraints before precise geometry, then inspect trade-offs between provisional configurations.

Scout: Rapid Exploration of Interface Layout Alternatives through High-Level Design Constraints ↗
DIS 2024 · Multimodal ideationDesignPrompt

Interaction: Compose a moodboard from images, colors, and text; change individual inputs to iteratively steer generative outcomes.

CAD transfer: Capture a partly understood design intention as editable multimodal evidence and let collaborators disambiguate it before commitment.

DesignPrompt: Using Multimodal Interaction for Design Exploration with Generative AI ↗
UIST 2023 · Creative codingSpellburst

Interaction: Explore creative code in branching nodes; prompt, edit code, adjust semantic controls, revisit and combine variants.

CAD transfer: Preserve parallel geometry variants and the instructions behind them, so unresolved alternatives remain inspectable across iterations.

Spellburst: A Node-based Interface for Exploratory Creative Coding with Natural Language Prompts ↗
CHI 2020 · Spatial co-designC-Space

Interaction: Combine abstract spatial diagrams, example retrieval, tangible manipulation, and AR prototyping to communicate incomplete collaborative spatial designs.

CAD transfer: Retain coarse shared representations and domain references while collaborators supply different kinds of information before geometry is finalized.

C-Space: An Interactive Prototyping Platform for Collaborative Spatial Design Exploration ↗
UIST 2023 · Literature sensemakingSynergi

Interaction: Provide seed papers or threads, expand along citations, group findings into editable themes, and synthesize sources.

CAD transfer: Start from a recognized design gap, expand traceable evidence and reorganize it into criteria without having the AI choose the final design.

Synergi: A Mixed-Initiative System for Scholarly Synthesis and Sensemaking ↗
UIST 2023 · Multilevel sensemakingSensecape

Interaction: Organize and navigate a spatial, multilevel information landscape, moving between detailed exploration and higher-level synthesis.

CAD transfer: Move from a known unresolved component-level choice to underlying tests, dependencies and evidence, then return to the system-level decision.

Sensecape: Enabling Multilevel Exploration and Sensemaking with Large Language Models ↗
UIST 2024 · Creative writingPatchview

Interaction: Move story elements and semantic magnets in a visual field to articulate vague worldbuilding intentions and correct the model's inferred relations.

CAD transfer: Externalize fuzzy relationships as spatially manipulable constraints, with explicit correction of tool assumptions rather than forcing premature precision.

Patchview: LLM-Powered Worldbuilding with Generative Dust and Magnet Visualization ↗
CHI 2024 · Programming educationCodeAid

Interaction: Request guidance and receive conceptual explanations, annotated pseudocode, or localized error hints without automatically disclosing a full solution.

CAD transfer: Offer graduated evidence or learning resources after a designer states a knowledge gap, while preserving decision ownership.

CodeAid: Evaluating a Classroom Deployment of an LLM-based Programming Assistant ↗
CHI 2025 · Writing feedbackFriction

Interaction: Segment extensive feedback, locate it with a text-linked heatmap, then stage reflection and revision planning.

CAD transfer: Anchor multidisciplinary critiques to geometry and support systematic triage of what is known, missing, conflicting, or deferred.

Friction: Deciphering Writing Feedback into Writing Revisions through LLM-Assisted Reflection ↗
UIST 2025 · Feedback synthesisSynthia

Interaction: Cluster and resize feedback bubbles, link them to text locations, select subsets, and compare candidate revisions.

CAD transfer: Aggregate component-linked comments without losing their source, then test alternative responses to conflicting feedback.

Synthia: Visually Interpreting and Synthesizing Feedback for Writing Revision ↗
CHI 2026 · Design historyDesignTrace

Interaction: Explore semantic design changes, make localized edits, branch design histories, and revisit earlier variants.

CAD transfer: Keep alternatives and their evolution recoverable when later evidence changes the criteria for an unresolved choice.

DesignTrace: Exploring, Iterating and Tracking Design Alternatives with GenAI ↗
SCF 2023 · CAD historyHistory in Motion

Interaction: Select model geometry to find the modeling features that produced it; animate their interactions in 3D and inspect or modify features through a control panel.

CAD transfer: Recover feature dependencies across handoffs and expose relevant model history when the rationale for a known decision is incomplete. Modeling history does not necessarily contain the author's design rationale.

History in Motion: Interactive 3D Animated Visualizations for Understanding and Exploring the Modeling History of 3D CAD Designs (full paper) ↗

Mechanism to brainstorm: mark a known open decision → record partial grounds and alternatives → gather traceable tests or contributions → compare, revise, or explicitly defer while preserving history.
