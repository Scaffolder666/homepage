Research agenda · Work in exploration

Design reasoning as designs evolve

Design goals, constraints, and decisions evolve together. I explore how interactive tools can help people notice, reconsider, and reason through design decisions.

Design decision space

Two dimensions guide this agenda: awareness of a decision and the ability to resolve it. Both depend on the decision and can change during design.

Not aware · Can resolve

Discover a decision

Relevant expertise exists, but an important decision has not yet been noticed.

Theory: Situated invention · Kinds of seeing · Breakdowns and creativity · Design fixation

Aware · Can resolve

Reason through a decision

Compare choices and reinterpret previous experience in the current situation.

Theory: Design rationale / QOC · Prior knowledge in design · Constructive memory

Not aware · Cannot resolve

Recognize a knowledge need

Notice a question and identify the knowledge needed to address it.

Research: Design knowledge needs · Design metacognition

Aware · Cannot resolve

Share an open decision

Bring together knowledge and perspectives that no individual designer possesses.

Theory: Symmetry of ignorance · Boundary objects · Boundary negotiating artifacts

Across the decision space

Design problems, solutions, and interpretations change together. Uncertainty can prompt reflection and further exploration, not only resolution.

Wicked problems · Problem–solution co-evolution · Situated FBS · Design metacognition · Uncertainty as a generative resource
