Privacy & Analytics

Website: shuofeng.org · Last updated: October 23, 2026

Vercel Web Analytics

This website uses Vercel Web Analytics to understand aggregate site traffic, such as popular pages and the countries where visits originate. Vercel describes its Web Analytics as cookie-free and aggregated rather than designed to identify individual visitors. Learn about Vercel's analytics privacy practices.

Optional Google Analytics

When enabled, Google Analytics 4 is offered as an optional service. It can provide aggregate website usage statistics, including approximate country, state or province, and city information derived from network location. It cannot reliably identify a visitor's neighborhood or street address.

The Google Analytics tag is not downloaded unless you choose Allow analytics. You can choose No thanks without affecting access to this website. You can also change your choice using the Analytics preferences control in the footer of public pages.

Google Analytics may use cookies when you agree. Visit Google's Privacy Policy for information about Google's processing. This website does not enable Google advertising personalization through its analytics tag.

Geolocation statistics are approximate and aggregated, and low visitor counts may be hidden or grouped by the analytics provider.
